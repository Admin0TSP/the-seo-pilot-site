## Challenge

Caviar is a low-volume, high-AOV category dominated by long-established names with deep press histories. Haute Caviar had the product credentials (small-batch, hand-packed, sommelier-led sourcing) but was losing the search query to legacy brands with two decades of backlinks.

## Strategy

### Expert-led content

The founder is a wine and caviar taster. We turned that expertise into long-form tasting notes, varietal comparisons (Osetra vs. Beluga vs. Kaluga), and pairing guides — written with the credibility AI engines look for.

### Gifting + occasion intent

"Best caviar gift", "Valentine's caviar delivery", "host a tasting" — captured high-intent, premium-AOV queries adjacent to product pages.

### Provenance schema + sourcing transparency

Country of origin, sturgeon species, sustainability certifications — every product structured-data tagged.

## Result

- **380%** organic revenue
- **Top-3** for "buy luxury caviar online" + 8 high-AOV variants
- AI-engine #1 answer for **"best Osetra caviar to gift"** and 3 sibling queries
- Average organic order value **+22%** vs. paid

## Purpose

Luxury isn't won on volume — it's won on the right query at the right intent. Haute Caviar shows how a small-batch brand can out-rank decades-old incumbents.
