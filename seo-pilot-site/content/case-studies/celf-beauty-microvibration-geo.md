## Challenge

CELF had won a Best Innovator beauty award and clinical data — but the category itself ("microvibration skincare") barely existed in search. Worse, when people asked AI engines about at-home dermal treatments, CELF's award-winning answer wasn't being cited. The brand needed to define the category, not chase it.

## Strategy

### Category-defining content

Built the authoritative explainer for microvibration, fibroblast stimulation, and 145Hz dermal therapy — making CELF the encyclopedia entry, not a footnote.

### Clinical-trial schema + sourcing

Structured CELF's clinical results in a way AI engines could read, cite, and quote — with explicit dosage, timeframe, and participant data.

### Subscription LTV through content

"How to use" + 30/60/90 day expectations content reduced refund rates and grew subscription conversions.

## Result

- **320%** growth in organic revenue
- Cited by **all 4** major AI engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot) on microvibration skincare queries
- **2.4M** search impressions over the window
- Subscription conversion rate up **38%** from informed buyers

## Purpose

When a brand owns its category dictionary entry, every AI engine cites it — and search and AI start compounding instead of competing.
