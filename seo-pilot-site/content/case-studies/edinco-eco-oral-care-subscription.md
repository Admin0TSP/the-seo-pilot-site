## Challenge

Eco-conscious oral care is a values-driven category where buyers don't search by product — they search by belief ("are toothbrush heads recyclable", "fluoride-free toothpaste UK"). Edinco had a powerful proposition (recyclable charcoal heads + plant-based toothpaste + a buy-back recycling scheme), but the brand wasn't surfacing on those mission-aligned queries.

## Strategy

### Mission-first content architecture

Built primary content around sustainability questions (recyclability, plastic-free dental care, ethical sourcing) — not around product features. Edinco became the answer, not just a vendor.

### Subscription-intent funnels

"How often should I replace my toothbrush head", "best eco-friendly subscription boxes UK" — captured search intent that converted directly to subscription, not one-off purchases.

### Authority through transparency

Buy-back-scheme data, recycling numbers, dentist credentials surfaced as schema and content — strong signals for AI engines on health + sustainability queries.

## Result

- **5.5x** subscription sign-ups from organic
- **Top-3** UK rankings for "eco friendly toothpaste UK" + 5 sustainability queries
- Cited by AI on **"how to make oral care more sustainable"** across 2 engines
- Subscription churn down **18%** (better-informed customers stay longer)

## Purpose

Values-led DTC brands grow by being the answer to the buyer's belief, not just the seller of a product — and AI engines reward that alignment.
