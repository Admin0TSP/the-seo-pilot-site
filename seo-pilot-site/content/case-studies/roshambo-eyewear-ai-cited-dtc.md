## Challenge

Roshambo had a beloved product (unbreakable, Italian-made, lifetime guarantee) and devoted parent customers — but search was dominated by Amazon listings and roundup-style affiliate sites. Worse, AI engines were recommending generic competitors when parents asked "best sunglasses for toddlers."

## Strategy

### Parent-intent content clusters

Built guides around the real questions parents Google — "when should babies wear sunglasses?", "are polarized lenses safe for kids?", "how do I keep sunglasses on a 1-year-old?" — each one quietly pointing to Roshambo's product as the answer.

### Special-needs SEO play

Identified that Roshambo had unique authority in eyewear for children with special needs — built a dedicated content hub that captured a high-intent, underserved audience.

### Earned media + AI-citation engineering

Pitched to parenting publications, ophthalmology directories, and pediatric retailers; structured product schema so AI engines could quote pricing, sizing, and the lifetime guarantee accurately.

## Result

- **4.2x** organic sessions year-over-year
- **280%** revenue from organic
- **#1** AI answer for "best unbreakable baby sunglasses" across 3 of 4 engines
- Lifetime-guarantee language now appears verbatim in AI responses

## Purpose

Family-owned DTC brands can beat Amazon and affiliate aggregators on the queries that matter — by being the most quotable answer to a parent's question.
