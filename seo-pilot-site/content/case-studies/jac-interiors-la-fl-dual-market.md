## Challenge

JAC operates in two markets — Los Angeles and Fort Lauderdale — but Google was treating them as one site, splitting authority and confusing the local pack. Affluent buyers in both cities were finding competitors first. The firm needed clear geographic signals without diluting the brand.

## Strategy

### Dual-metro site architecture

Separate, properly-linked LA and Fort Lauderdale hubs with localised contact info, NAP citations, and metro-specific portfolio.

### Service-page depth, not breadth

Instead of 30 thin service pages per metro, 8 deep ones (kitchen, bathroom, design-build, commercial, etc.) with full project galleries.

### High-end editorial earned media

Featured in Houzz Pro, regional lifestyle press, and design directories — anchored to the right metro page in each placement.

## Result

- **Top-3** local pack in both LA and Fort Lauderdale for "interior designer" + 5 service variations
- **3.8x** RFP and design-intent form submissions
- Featured on **Houzz Pro** + 4 regional design publications

## Purpose

Multi-location services brands often fight themselves on Google. This shows how to give each metro the architecture it needs to win locally — without splitting the brand.
