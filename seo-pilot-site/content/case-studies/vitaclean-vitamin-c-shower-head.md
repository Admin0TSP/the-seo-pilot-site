## Challenge

Filtered shower heads is a saturated DTC category dominated by US-priced competitors. VitaClean had a sharper UK positioning (female-founded, charity-tied) but was losing UK search to Amazon listings and US imports with no UK shipping clarity.

## Strategy

### UK-first localisation

Pricing in GBP, UK delivery clarity, UK water-quality content (London hard water, Manchester chlorine levels) — every page geo-anchored.

### Concern-based content

"Shower head for eczema", "shower head for dry hair", "is tap water bad for skin" — captured queries from people describing the problem, not the product.

### Mission-led trust signals

Founder story, charity tie-in, female-founded credentials surfaced as authority signals AI engines weight heavily on health-adjacent queries.

## Result

- **4.8x** organic sessions
- **Top-3** UK rankings for "vitamin C shower head" + 7 concern-based queries
- **220%** organic revenue lift
- AI-engine answer for "best shower head for hard water UK" across 3 engines

## Purpose

Geo-specific DTC brands win by being the local answer — not by competing globally on the same query.
