## Challenge

Ziab's audience is one of the most technical and lowest-volume on the internet — research scientists searching for single-domain antibodies, nanobody sequences, and AI-designed bindings. Generic SEO playbooks don't work. Every keyword has hundreds of monthly searches at most, but each click can become a six-figure RFQ.

## Strategy

### Protein-target landing pages

Programmatic pages for each protein target (PSD95, EGFR, CD20, etc.) with the antibody catalog, methodology, and order links — each page becomes an RFQ-capture asset.

### Method-paper-grade content

"How AI designs nanobody sequences", "single-domain vs. conventional antibodies", "in silico screening pipeline" — written for a PhD reader, indexed for Google Scholar discoverability.

### ResearchGate + lab-directory citations

Built citation network across research-community sites — high-authority signal for both Google and AI engines that index scholarly content.

## Result

- **12x** organic traffic on research-intent keywords
- **47** qualified RFQs from organic in 9 months
- **6** university-lab procurement contracts attributed to organic
- Cited by **Perplexity Pro** on 14 research-domain prompts

## Purpose

Low-volume, high-value B2B SEO is a different discipline — measured in RFQs, not sessions. This shows how to do it for a deep-tech audience.
