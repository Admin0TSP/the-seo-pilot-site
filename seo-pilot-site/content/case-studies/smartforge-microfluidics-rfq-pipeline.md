## Challenge

SmartForge sells microfluidic and medical-device precision molding to one of the most technical buyer audiences imaginable — R&D engineers and lab leads at medical device startups. They needed organic to feed real RFQs, not just impressions. The queries (e.g., "rapid microfluidic prototyping") have tiny volumes but enormous deal-size potential.

## Strategy

### Capability × industry matrix

Pages for each combination of capability (precision molding, polymer bonding, optical-grade finishing) × industry (microfluidics, drug delivery, diagnostics, point-of-care).

### Speed-and-cost claims with evidence

"20x faster to market, 10x cheaper" — backed by case-by-case process explainers that engineers actually trusted.

### Engineer-grade content

CAD-tolerance ranges, polymer compatibility tables, regulatory considerations — written for the buyer's persona (medical-device R&D lead), not for marketers.

## Result

- **38** qualified RFQs from organic in 10 months
- **Top-5** for "microfluidic prototyping" + "medical device rapid molding" + 6 sibling queries
- **3** RFQs closed into multi-quarter manufacturing agreements
- Average RFQ deal value **2.4x** vs. trade-show-sourced RFQs

## Purpose

Specialty B2B manufacturing is one of the most under-served corners of SEO. SmartForge proves real revenue follows when the content speaks to engineers in their own language.
