## Challenge

Defined Sleep is the only CBD sleep brand to run double-blind, placebo-controlled, randomized human trials — but that clinical authority wasn't showing up in search or in AI answers. When people asked ChatGPT about CBD for sleep, generic, untested brands were getting recommended.

## Strategy

### Trial-data-as-content

Turned the clinical study into a primary SEO asset — structured around methodology, sample size, statistical significance, and participant outcomes. Quotable. Citable. Defensible.

### Restorative sleep entity ownership

Built the canonical guides for "restorative sleep", "deep + REM sleep extension", and "how CBD interacts with sleep architecture" — claiming the underlying topic graph.

### Compliance-first GEO

Every claim sourced, every dosage stated, every contraindication disclosed — so AI engines could quote without hallucination risk.

## Result

- **6x** brand citation rate across AI engines vs. baseline
- Top-10 rankings for "clinically tested sleep supplement" + 11 related queries
- **290%** organic sessions
- **2.1x** organic checkout conversion from buyers arriving better-informed

## Purpose

If you've done the harder, more expensive thing (clinical trials), AI engines should reward you — but only if your data is structured to be quoted. This shows how.
